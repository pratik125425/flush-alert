# Surface: Flush Alert explainer + replay (site/index.html)

Mode: Read (explainer page) with one embedded Operate tool (the replay). Audience: Xylem judges and engineers reviewing many entries quickly, on laptops in daylight and sometimes on phones. The job is to understand the blind spot, trust the backtest, and see that it scales. The one action: scrub the replay and open a site.

Copy rule (user, binding): state the adaptive-sampling result as a finding ("timing beats volume"), lead with the strengths, keep limitations short and factual. Never overstate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A field report you can replay. It reads like a government data bulletin set by a newsroom graphics desk, with every number sourced and dated. It refuses the dashboard-of-cards and the AI-landing hero.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with cool slate ink (#18232e), river blue (#1d5c87) for every data mark, pale blue-gray wash for the river and OK states, and vermilion (#c43d12) only when an alert has fired. Source Serif 4 headlines (display optical size, upright, no italics), Public Sans for body, UI and tabular numerals. Separation by hairline rules and whitespace, never shadowed cards. Square corners.

STORY: Drought hides nitrate in the soil, the first wet spring flushes it, and monthly samples miss it. A flow-only rule flags those springs weeks ahead. Timing beats volume. It runs on 1,000+ gauges with no sensor.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A masthead strip (name, "Historical backtest" label, team), then a large serif sentence about the June 12, 2025 watering ban with the post-drought error figure set inside the sentence at display size, a short deck, and below it the hero chart beginning, true nitrate in blue against monthly dots. The reader scrolls; there are no buttons.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). The brief's own words: "like a government data portal crossed with a top newsroom graphics desk"; "editorial and data-first. A restrained palette (deep river blue/slate, one alert color...), a serious serif or grotesk for headings... real numbers in large type, and source lines under every chart", plus the page structure, sections 1 to 8.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the replay date stamp in large tabular numerals beside the map; sites change state as the scrubber passes, and the single active alert pulses.
